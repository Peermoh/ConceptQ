Number Town and Story Library

Two new courses beside Python. Maths builds Number Town, one district per grade. English builds the Story Library, one wing per grade. Every lesson builds something real in its place, with Nibb teaching on the chalkboard, fresh quizzes and flashcards, just like the Python house. Topics follow the NCERT books in their order; the stories and examples are our own. Grades 2 to 5 are planned lesson by lesson below and get built first; grades 6 to 10 are planned by topic.

Five choices to confirm before I write lessons
  1. Our own stories, NCERT's topics (recommended). Each lesson teaches what its NCERT chapter teaches, in the same order, but with our own examples and characters. Copying textbook stories and exercises is not allowed, and our own versions can be tied to the town and library.
  2. A young-learner mode for grades 2 and 3. Nibb reads every question aloud, any word can be tapped to hear it, many answers are pictures, steps are shorter (5 or 6), and buttons are bigger.
  3. One app, three courses. The Learn tab gets a course switch: Python, Maths, English. Each course has its own path, progress, place to build and flashcard deck. Embers, the streak and the prize wheel are shared.
  4. Speaking practice is listen-and-repeat. The app cannot use the microphone, so English speaking steps play a model line, the learner says it aloud, then taps "I said it". Reading and listening are fully checked.
  5. Build order. First the course switch, then Grade 2 Maths and English together (23 lessons), then grades 3, 4 and 5, publishing after each batch so you can test as it grows. Grades 6 to 10 come after you have tried the primary grades.
