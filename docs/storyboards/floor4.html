Floor 4 storyboard: the rooftop garden

The house is finished. Expert builds what grows around it: a garden with a shed, water and lights, and a roof terrace on the flat garage roof with an antenna to the outside world. It goes beyond the PCEP and PCAP exams and follows the Python tutorial chapters 9–12. It is built in the order a real garden is: shed and tools, water, lights, a gate, plant labels, a meter, sprinklers, an antenna, then the smart garden.

Four choices to confirm before I write lessons
  1. Build on the garage roof and in the garden, not on the house roof (recommended). The pitched roof already has solar panels and the weather station. A flat garage roof is where a real roof terrace goes.
  2. Split generators and the standard library (recommended). Iterators and generators get one lesson each, and the standard library tour becomes two: "everyday tools" (os, sys.argv, datetime, decimal, zlib) and "regular expressions". That makes 11 lessons plus the Master Builder challenge.
  3. The internet lesson is simulated in the app. A pretend weather service answers with real JSON, so it works offline and never sends anything anywhere. The code is exactly what you would use with the real requests library.
  4. Garage project: the smart garden. It reads a forecast (JSON), waters each bed at the same time (async), logs what it did, uses dataclasses for the plants, and has tests. This is the weather app from the curriculum, finished properly.
