Floor 2 storyboard

The stairs from the Loops lesson lead up to bare ground. Each lesson builds the next real piece of the second floor, in the order a builder would, and earns one living item you can tap to see the idea at work. Covers PCEP sections 3–5 in full.

Three choices to confirm before I write lessons
  1. Split Lists and Functions into two lessons each (recommended). Each is too big for one 8–9 step lesson without rushing. That makes 11 lessons plus a boss, instead of the 9 in the curriculum doc.
  2. The roof comes off first. A crane lifts the ground-floor roof in lesson 1 and puts it back on top of floor 2 at Sets, when the walls are up. Nothing appears before what holds it up.
  3. Garage project after the boss: a to-do list that saves to a file, built over a few short sessions, using every floor-2 idea.
